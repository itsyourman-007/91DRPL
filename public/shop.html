The process

How DAB works

A straightforward four-stage routine that fits into any existing chairside workflow — no extra training required.

01 — Mount

Fit before the procedure

Clip a fresh DAB onto the handpiece in under two minutes. No tools, no modification to your existing setup.

02 — Seal

Forms a working seal

The soft-edge collar sits around the treatment site, keeping spray and splatter from escaping into the room.

03 — Work as usual

Drill, scale or polish

DAB stays in place through the procedure while it contains aerosol at the source, patient after patient.

04 — Dispose

Discard, no reprocessing

Remove and bin the entire unit as clinical waste. No autoclaving, no cleaning — a fresh DAB for the next patient.

Common questions

Before you order

Which handpieces does DAB fit?
DAB is designed for standard friction-grip and latch-type dental handpieces across major manufacturer lines. If you use a less common unit, write to us before ordering to confirm compatibility.
Is DAB a single-use device?
Yes. Each DAB is fitted for one patient and discarded as clinical waste afterward — no autoclaving, no reprocessing, no cartridge replacement.
How many packs do I need?
Each pack has 12 pieces. A rough guide is one DAB per aerosol-generating procedure — busier practices typically order a few packs at a time.
How does delivery work?
Orders are shipped pan-India in protective packaging, typically arriving within 3–5 business days of payment confirmation.
How do I pay?
Payment is by UPI — scan the QR shown at checkout with any UPI app. It pays directly into 91DAB's account, and your order is confirmed once the reference number is verified.
